ViralTrack: three design directions

Design proposals for the owner. Nothing here is implemented in ViralTrack or in the foundation dashboard.

All values are synthetic DEMO data. Only approved metrics are shown: raw views, likes, comments, dates, length and follower counts, plus the allowed engagement rate (omitted when likes are hidden). Sorting is limited to Most views and Newest.

A · Premium AnalyticsLight-first, information-dense: KPI tiles, category and weekday charts, a sortable-by-policy table with provenance footer. Dark theme toggle. B · Creator IntelligenceDark-first discovery grid with large 16:9 thumbnails, two numbers per card, a quick-view drawer with RAW/DERIVED labels, loading skeleton and empty state. C · Futuristic IntelligenceGlass panels, an ambient network field in the hero only, animated counters and an explorable niche map. Motion toggle and reduced-motion support.

Recommended: B for Discover and the shell, A for Analytics and tables, C's niche map only as a lazy-loaded exploration view. See ../DESIGN_CONCEPT_COMPARISON.md.
